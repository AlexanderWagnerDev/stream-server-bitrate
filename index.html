1 · Streaming sources & fallback

Servers are tried in order. The first server with a valid positive bitrate is used. Private stats keys stay in your browser and downloaded file — never publish that file.

2 · Widgets & display order

Turn individual metrics on/off and move them up or down. Unavailable statistics are automatically hidden. Graph history stays in browser memory only.

Every visible metric label can be renamed, translated, or hidden by leaving its field empty. Numbers remain live; no text is baked into the design.
